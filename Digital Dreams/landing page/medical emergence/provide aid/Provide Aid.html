    • Provide Aid

    Offer immediate assistance to individuals during medical emergencies with these essential first aid actions.

    💜
    CPR

    Perform chest compressions and rescue breaths if the person is unresponsive and not breathing.

    🧍‍♂️
    Place in Recovery Position

    Position the person on their side to keep their airway clear if they are breathing but not fully conscious.

    ⚡
    Use an AED

    Apply an automated external defibrillator if available for someone experiencing sudden cardiac arrest.

    💧
    Control Bleeding

    Apply pressure to the wound with a clean cloth to stop or slow bleeding.
