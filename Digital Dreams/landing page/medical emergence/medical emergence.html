What to Do in a Medical Emergency

Call for Help
Use the SOS button to notify your caregivers immediately.
Stay Calm
Try to remain calm and assess the situation.
Provide Aid
If possible, offer first aid according to your training.
Share Location
Ensure caregivers know your exact location for quick assist.
